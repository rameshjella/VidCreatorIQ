The city sleeps under a silver rain. Detective Mira walks through neon reflections, searching for a missing violinist.

Inside a quiet jazz club, the lights flicker as an old melody starts by itself. Mira discovers a torn music sheet with a hidden map.

She follows the map to an abandoned subway station where murals come alive with memories of the missing artist.

At dawn, Mira reaches the rooftop observatory. The violinist appears, playing one final song that reveals the truth behind the citywide blackout.

As the sun rises, the city powers back on, and Mira closes the case with a smile, listening to the last echo of the violin.
